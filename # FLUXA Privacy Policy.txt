# FLUXA Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

FLUXA ("we", "our", or "the App") is a personal flight-tracking application that helps users search for flights, track flight information, save favorite flights, explore nearby airports, and view local weather conditions.

This Privacy Policy explains how FLUXA handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Access and Process

### 1.1 Location Information

FLUXA may request access to your device's location to provide location-related features, including identifying your city, displaying local weather, and finding nearby airports.

Depending on the feature being used, the App may access your device's latitude and longitude.

- Your city name is sent to our weather service to retrieve weather information.
- Your latitude and longitude are sent to our flight-data service when you request nearby-airport information.
- Location access depends on your device permissions.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may not function correctly without it.

### 1.2 Flight Search and Tracking Information

When you use FLUXA to search for or track flights, the App sends the relevant search parameters to external flight-data services to retrieve flight information.

Depending on the feature, these parameters may include flight identifiers, airport codes, departure and arrival information, or route details.

This information is used to provide the flight-search, tracking, route-information, and nearby-airport features.

FLUXA is not an airline and does not operate aircraft. Flight information is provided by third-party services and may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate.

### 1.3 Weather Information

FLUXA uses WeatherAPI to retrieve current weather conditions.

When you request weather information, the App sends the relevant city name and the required API authentication parameter to WeatherAPI.

The weather information returned by the service is used to display weather conditions within FLUXA.

### 1.4 Information Stored on Your Device

FLUXA uses local device storage to retain certain app preferences and flight-related information.

Depending on your use of the App, locally stored information may include:

- Favorite flights.
- Tracked flights.
- Flight history or previously viewed flight information.
- Display preferences, including theme settings.

This information is stored using local application storage mechanisms, including SharedPreferences.

Local storage allows the App to preserve selected information between sessions. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored information.

We have not identified an account-based synchronization system in the code reviewed for this policy. Accordingly, this policy does not promise that your locally stored information is backed up or synchronized across devices.

### 1.5 Notifications

FLUXA supports local notifications, subject to your device's notification permissions.

The App may schedule notifications containing general flight-related tips. Notification functionality may depend on your device settings and operating system.

The notification implementation reviewed for this policy does not demonstrate that the contents of these tips are transmitted to a remote notification service.

You can manage or disable notifications through your device settings.

## 2. Third-Party Services

FLUXA relies on third-party services to provide certain features.

### AirLabs

AirLabs provides flight-related information used by features such as flight searches, route lookups, and nearby-airport searches.

Relevant search parameters, including flight identifiers, airport information, or location coordinates when required by a feature, may be sent to AirLabs.

Website: https://airlabs.co/

### WeatherAPI

WeatherAPI provides weather information. The App sends the requested city name and API authentication parameter to retrieve current weather conditions.

Website: https://www.weatherapi.com/

These third-party providers process requests according to their own terms and privacy practices. Their handling of information is subject to their respective policies.

FLUXA does not control the privacy practices of external service providers. Users should review the relevant provider policies for further information.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or processed by FLUXA is used to:

- Search for flights and retrieve flight information.
- Support flight tracking and display flight updates.
- Save and display favorite flights and flight history.
- Identify nearby airports when requested.
- Display weather conditions for the relevant city.
- Preserve app preferences and settings.
- Provide local notification functionality.
- Maintain the operation and reliability of the App.

We do not claim to use this information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

## 4. Advertising

The version of FLUXA covered by this draft does not intentionally display advertisements.

If advertising services are introduced in a future version, this policy and the relevant Google Play declarations will be updated as necessary to reflect the resulting data practices.

## 5. Data Sharing

FLUXA sends information to third-party services when necessary to provide requested features, as described in this policy.

For example, flight-search parameters may be sent to AirLabs, while a city name may be sent to WeatherAPI.

We do not claim that all information remains exclusively on your device, because some features require external API requests.

The code reviewed for this draft does not establish that FLUXA operates its own user-account database or sells personal information to third parties. This statement should be reviewed against the complete production app and any third-party SDKs before publication.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Certain preferences and flight-related information are stored locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored application data by clearing FLUXA's storage through your device settings or by uninstalling the App. The precise effect may depend on your device and operating system.

Information sent to third-party providers may be subject to their own retention and deletion practices. FLUXA cannot independently guarantee deletion of records held by those providers.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to design the App to handle information appropriately.

However, no application, network transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

FLUXA uses external API services to retrieve flight and weather information. API credentials and service configurations are managed as part of the application's technical implementation.

Users should keep their devices updated and use appropriate device-security settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy

FLUXA is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not intentionally request children's personal information for account registration through the functionality reviewed for this policy.

If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that requires action under applicable law, please contact us using the details below.

The intended age group and any applicable requirements should be confirmed before publication.

## 9. Your Choices and Permissions

You can exercise the following choices through the App or your device settings, where applicable:

- Deny or revoke location permission.
- Disable notifications.
- Clear locally stored application data.
- Stop using the App or uninstall it.

Revoking a permission may limit the availability of related features.

Requests concerning information held by third-party providers may need to be directed to those providers.
